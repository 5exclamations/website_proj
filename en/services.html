Services

From neuropsychological evaluations to innovative therapies like BAC Therapy, we provide a range of services designed to address developmental, emotional, and neurological challenges.

Neuropsychological Assessments

Assessment of cognitive, emotional and behavioural functions helps inform individual recommendations. It does not replace examination by a clinician authorised to make a medical diagnosis.

Bio-Acoustic Brain Correction

A non-invasive method that converts EEG signals into sound in real time. Suitability, goals and limitations are assessed individually before a course begins.

Neuropsychological correction

• Delay in psycho-speech development
• Autism spectrum disorder
• Behavioral disorders
• Attention deficit and hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
• Neurodynamic disorders
• Epilepsy
• Severe multiple developmental disorders
• Down syndrome

Transcranial micropolarization using the N.Yu. Kozhushko method

A supportive method considered only after professional assessment and discussion of goals and possible contraindications.
