What is BAC Therapy?

During bioacoustic correction, EEG data is converted into audible signals in real time. The procedure is non-invasive. Possible goals, limitations and individual suitability are discussed before a course begins.

How does it work?

Sensors record electrical brain activity and a computer system converts the EEG data into audible signals. The method may be considered as one part of a broader support or rehabilitation plan.

Step 1

Sensors are placed on the patient’s head to monitor brain activity (EEG).

Step 2

This data is converted into acoustic waves – the brain’s unique “music.”

Step 3

The patient listens to this sound in real time, encouraging the brain to self-correct through feedback.

Who can benefit

Children (1+ y.o.)

  • Developmental delays (speech or mental)
  • Autism spectrum disorders
  • ADHD and behavioral challenges
  • Neurological issues like tics and enuresis
  • Recovery from brain injuries or surgeries

Adults

  • Stress, chronic fatigue, post-traumatic stress syndrome
  • Emotional disorders like anxiety, depression, mood instability
  • Neurological recovery after strokes or brain trauma
  • Sleep, appetite, and cognitive impairments
